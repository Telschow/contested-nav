# ADR-0036: B1 is closed as characterised, not solved

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10
- Related: [ADR-0003](0003-ship-visual-disabled.md), [ADR-0027](0027-how-the-synthetic-runs-are-initialised.md), [ADR-0028](0028-the-b1-gate.md), [ADR-0030](0030-the-gate-for-the-next-visual-update-candidate.md), [ADR-0031](0031-an-adaptive-visual-noise-scale.md), [ADR-0032](0032-the-adaptive-visual-scale-takes-every-visual-update.md), [ADR-0033](0033-one-convention-for-the-synthetic-visual-translation-noise.md), [ADR-0034](0034-a-gauss-markov-model-of-the-correlated-visual-error.md), [issue #44](https://github.com/Telschow/contested-nav/issues/44), [issue #113](https://github.com/Telschow/contested-nav/issues/113)

## Context

B1 is the question whether camera-based motion tracking can be switched on when GNSS is denied without the filter becoming confidently wrong. [ADR-0028](0028-the-b1-gate.md) fixed a gate for it in advance. The gate was run once, on the held-out seeds 100 to 124, and the first candidate failed it on the conservative side, on the fixture before [ADR-0033](0033-one-convention-for-the-synthetic-visual-translation-noise.md). The result was accepted and not tuned away.

[ADR-0030](0030-the-gate-for-the-next-visual-update-candidate.md) then fixed a robust gate in two scenarios for any new candidate. The adaptive visual noise scale ([ADR-0031](0031-an-adaptive-visual-noise-scale.md), [ADR-0032](0032-the-adaptive-visual-scale-takes-every-visual-update.md)) and the Gauss-Markov model ([ADR-0034](0034-a-gauss-markov-model-of-the-correlated-visual-error.md)) were each tuned once by the procedure their ADR fixed in advance, and each failed on the development seeds. No held-out block was used for either.

**Where the evidence is.** MEASUREMENT, all of it generated and checked against committed CSVs, none retyped here. The decision run of the first candidate is in [the held-out section](../calibration.md#the-b1-gate-the-decision-run-on-the-held-out-seeds) of the calibration page. The two scenarios and the plain clone are in [the candidate gate](../calibration.md#the-candidate-gate-of-adr-0030-on-the-development-seeds). The two tuning runs are in [the adaptive scale](../calibration.md#the-tuning-run-of-the-adaptive-visual-scale-adr-0031) and [the Gauss-Markov model](../calibration.md#the-tuning-run-of-the-gauss-markov-visual-error-model-adr-0034), the latter with a diagnostic at other correlation times. A last diagnostic, [the usefulness in S2 with a near-exact model](../calibration.md#usefulness-in-scenario-s2-with-a-near-exact-model-diagnostic), is generated by `navkit gate usefulness-s2`.

**What the last diagnostic adds.** On the development seeds, a model close to the simulator's own error process holds the three NEES conditions in S2 and still does not meet the usefulness conditions: the candidate is better than the control in too few seeds, and in some seeds it is much worse. Making the model more exact did not change which conditions hold. This is one near-exact model on the development seeds of the diagnostic, so it does not show that no estimator can meet those conditions; it shows that these did not.

## Options

(a) Close B1 as characterised, and keep the tools. Chosen. The question was asked with a pre-registered gate, the candidates were tried by their pre-registered procedures, and what they show is recorded.

(b) Amend the gate so that usefulness is required only in S1, and try the Gauss-Markov model on a held-out block. Rejected for now. It would be the third reshaping of the experiment, after the noise convention of ADR-0033 and the gate statistic of ADR-0030, and the model is not robust to the correlation time, so a pass would not justify switching vision on.

(c) Build more candidates. Rejected for now. The tuning runs and the diagnostics point at the correlation of the errors, and a further candidate would need a new ADR that says what it adds.

## Decision

B1 is closed as characterised, not solved. Work towards a default-on configuration for visual fusion stops here. The shipped configuration does not change: vision_enabled stays False, the single-anchor model stays the default and the control row, and the stochastic clone, the adaptive visual noise scale and the Gauss-Markov model stay opt-in.

What is recorded as known. First, the single anchor is overconfident under GNSS denial and does not recover from a start attitude error inside its own declared prior. Second, the stochastic clone passes the S1 gate of ADR-0030 on development seeds and fails the S2 gate. Third, temporally correlated measurement errors are absorbed into the state, so the innovations do not reveal them and an adaptive scale cannot calibrate them; the adaptive scale failed on development seeds. Fourth, a Gauss-Markov model of the correlated error restores calibration in S2 when its correlation time matches the data and is conservative or overconfident when it does not; it failed on development seeds. Fifth, on the development seeds the usefulness conditions of the gate are not met in S2 even by a near-exact model, so a configuration cannot pass the gate as written by calibration alone. Sixth, the held-out block of seeds 100 to 124 was used once, under ADR-0028, and the blocks from seed 125 are unused and stay unused.

What stays. The gate, the ledger of held-out blocks and the tuning commands stay in the repository as tools, and every table that reports these results stays generated and checked. No further candidate is built under ADR-0030 without a new ADR that states what it adds to this record.

## Consequences

- `CONSTRAINTS.md` B1 stays listed as a known blocker, with the status "open, work stopped (ADR-0036)". The held-out status block there stays generated from its CSV.
- The README table of what the evidence shows, the status page, the roadmap and the calibration page say the same, and every claim in them is tied to a committed CSV by a test.
- The issues [#44](https://github.com/Telschow/contested-nav/issues/44) and [#113](https://github.com/Telschow/contested-nav/issues/113) are closed by the maintainer with a pointer to this ADR.
- The stretch ADRs ([#116](https://github.com/Telschow/contested-nav/issues/116), [#117](https://github.com/Telschow/contested-nav/issues/117), [#52](https://github.com/Telschow/contested-nav/issues/52)) stay open as independent work.
- `vision_enabled` stays False. Changing that is a separate maintainer decision and is not made here.
